## What’s new since 0.8.1

- Group and move clips, close track gaps, use the main-track magnet, and add named or detected beat markers.
- Adjust clip speed, reverse footage, generate optical flow, stabilize camera motion, and animate position, rotation, scale, opacity, and audio gain.
- Record voiceover, generate narration with eSpeak NG, normalize loudness, and adjust pitch and stereo channels.
- Generate editable English or bilingual captions with a multilingual Whisper model. Export a single caption track or remove a transcript segment across the timeline.
- Remove backgrounds and reframe subjects with a local U²-Net model. Apply overlay motion tracking, blend modes, LUTs, motion blur, and automatic color correction.
- Recover unsaved drafts, reopen recent projects, export a timeline range, embed an MP4 cover and title, and export GIF, MP3, or FLAC.

## What’s missing

- Multicamera editing, compound clips, dedicated proxies, editable speed ramps, and richer effect animation.
- AI object removal, advanced retouching and relighting, voice isolation, general caption translation beyond English, dubbing, and lip sync.
- Automatic short-form edits, generative media and presenters, cloud sync, team review, and direct social publishing.
- CapCut’s proprietary catalog and hosted services are outside CutAd’s independent implementation. See the [workflow inventory](https://github.com/ArthurSimin/CutAd/blob/v1.0.0-beta.2/CAPCUT_WORKFLOWS.md) for the detailed coverage and limitations.

This is a prerelease. Offline speech and subject tools require separately installed runtimes and compatible models.
